Glass

Frosted surfaces that blur what sits behind them. Each screen has a solid base plus two or three soft glows so the blur has something to show.

GlassFill white 6%, border white 12%, blur 24, saturation 140%. Cards and panels on dark.Tailwind: glass
Glass strongFill white 10%, border white 18%, blur 32. Modals, receipts, drawers, the cart.Tailwind: glass-strong
Gold glassFill gold 14%, border gold 45%. Selected rows and the one suggested action.Tailwind: glass-gold
Light glassFill white 68%, blur 24. Back Office cards and toolbars.Tailwind: glass-light
Data glassFill white 84%. Tables and long text, so reading never fights the blur.Tailwind: glass-data

Rules that keep glass usable

Never stack more than two glass layers. Long text and tables sit on data glass, at 84% fill or more. Gold is never a glass fill except the gold glass tint. Buttons stay solid. Without blur support, or with reduced transparency on, every glass becomes its solid graphite fallback.

          backdropFilter: 'blur(24px) saturate(140%)',
