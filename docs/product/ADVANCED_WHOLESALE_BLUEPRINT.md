## Purpose

Advanced Wholesale extends the Production-validated Basic Wholesale foundation with sales orders, payment terms, partial fulfillment, receivables, credit limits, invoices, and returns or credits. It must not create a second inventory pool. Retail, Wholesale, Dealer, Sales Orders, and invoice fulfillment continue to use the same tenant, branch, product-variant balance and immutable inventory ledger.

Branch transfers and restock remain inventory operations and never become wholesale revenue.

## Recommended locked behavior

### Customer eligibility

- Every sales order requires an active reseller or wholesale customer.
- Customer, price list, payment terms, and credit settings are tenant-scoped and resolved by the server.
- A client-supplied tenant ID is never trusted.

### Sales-order lifecycle

```text
draft -> confirmed -> partially_fulfilled -> fulfilled
                   -> cancelled
```

- Draft orders may be edited and do not reserve inventory.
- Confirming an order snapshots the customer, branch, price list, line description, SKU, quantity, unit price, discount, tax, and totals.
- A confirmed order is corrected through cancellation of the remaining quantity or explicit amendment history, never destructive editing.
- Cancellation never deletes the order or its audit history.

### Shared inventory and reservation

- Confirmation creates branch-variant reservations but does not reduce on-hand stock or create revenue.
- `available = on_hand - reserved`; Retail POS and other channels see the reduced available quantity.
- Fulfillment consumes the reservation and posts the normal sale and inventory movement atomically.
- Partial fulfillment releases only the fulfilled reserved quantity; the unfulfilled balance remains reserved.
- Cancelling a remaining quantity releases its reservation without creating a sale.
- Overselling is rejected inside the atomic database command.

### Fulfillment and invoices

- Every fulfillment creates one immutable invoice and one linked sale snapshot.
- An order may therefore have multiple invoices when fulfilled in parts.
- Invoice numbers are unique per tenant and generated by the server.
- Invoice totals use integer centavos in TypeScript and `numeric` in PostgreSQL.
- Returns use the existing sale-reversal and inventory-return controls and append a linked credit note.

### Payment terms and receivables

- Recommended terms: `prepaid`, `cod`, `net_7`, `net_15`, and `net_30`.
- `prepaid` and `cod` require full payment at fulfillment.
- Net terms create an invoice receivable with a server-calculated due date.
- Receivable balances come only from append-only invoice, payment-allocation, credit-note, and adjustment ledgers.
- Partial payments are allowed and allocated explicitly to invoices.
- Overpayments are not silently applied; they require a separate customer-credit entry.

### Credit controls

- Each reseller may have a tenant-currency credit limit. `0` means no credit is available, not unlimited credit.
- Available credit is `credit_limit - open_receivables - confirmed_unfulfilled_order_exposure`.
- Order confirmation and fulfillment both recheck the limit atomically.
- A credit-limit override requires an approval record with actor, reason, amount, expiry, audit event, and outbox event.

### Authorization and entitlement

- `advanced_wholesale` must be enabled before any Advanced Wholesale endpoint or page is usable.
- Owners and authorized managers may manage orders and terms.
- Cashiers may fulfill only when explicitly granted and assigned to the location.
- RLS remains defense in depth; Hyperdrive receives execute access only to narrow command functions.

### AW2 - Partial fulfillment and invoices

### AW3 - Terms, credit, and receivables

- Customer payment-term and credit-limit settings
- Due dates, open balances, aging, and payment allocation
- Credit-limit enforcement and controlled approval override

### AW4 - Returns and credits

- Credit notes linked to invoice and sale reversal
- Return-to-stock or non-restock disposition
- Receivable reduction and payment/refund reconciliation
- End-to-end staging UAT and Production release gate

## Required validation

- Retail and wholesale channels consume one shared branch-variant inventory position.
- A draft order changes neither reservation nor stock.
- Confirm is atomic and idempotent; retries do not double-reserve.
- Partial fulfillment cannot exceed ordered, reserved, or available quantities.
- Cancellation releases only the unfulfilled reservation.
- Fulfillment posts one sale, invoice, inventory movement set, audit event, and outbox event.
- Cross-tenant customer, location, product, price-list, order, invoice, or payment references are rejected.
- Credit exposure and receivable balances reconcile after payments, refunds, and credit notes.
- No browser role or general API login receives direct business-table DML.
